Why optimized cloud AI wins on margin

Unoptimized competitors pay a growing server bill on every single test. RespiCore's optimized cloud inference pipeline keeps cost per screening low and predictable, even as volume scales.

At 1,000,000 screenings, RespiCore's optimized cloud pipeline holds at ~$0.003 marginal cost, while unoptimized cloud-dependent tools stay pinned to $0.02–$0.10 per call — the gap that funds a >85% gross margin.
